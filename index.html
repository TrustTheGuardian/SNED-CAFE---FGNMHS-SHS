Welcome to SNED CAFE

Welcome to SNED CAFE, the official coffee shop and pastry corner of FGNMHS Senior High School. We believe that great ideas are born over great coffee. Our mission is to provide students, teachers, and guests with premium, handcrafted beverages and freshly baked pastries in a warm, welcoming environment.

Every cup we serve is made with love, and every pastry is crafted to perfection. Whether you need a quick morning boost before class, a cozy spot to study, or a place to unwind, SNED CAFE is here for you.

Rationale

SNED Café was established in response to the challenges of providing accessible and meaningful Work Immersion opportunities for learners with special educational needs. The required 360-hour Work Immersion, limited SNEd teachers, and concerns of external industries regarding accessibility, communication, safety, and supervision created barriers to meaningful workplace placement.

Established in December 2023, SNED Café initially operated as a simple booth and later developed into a physical school-based café and simulated workplace. It provides learners with a safe and supportive environment where they can develop soft skills such as communication, teamwork, responsibility, and work ethics; hard skills such as food preparation, sanitation, customer service, and café operations; and technological skills through digital tools, financial recording, and basic web development.

The café also provides authentic community and workplace experiences through school and Division-level events, benchmarking activities, and interactions with nearby businesses. Its income supports learning materials and learner needs, while the skills gained help prepare learners for employment, entrepreneurship, and greater independence. Thus, SNED Café serves as a sustainable and inclusive approach to Work Immersion that transforms workplace barriers into meaningful opportunities for learning, participation, productivity, and empowerment.

General Objectives

General Objective: To establish and sustain an inclusive, accessible, and sustainable school-based simulated workplace that provides SNED learners with meaningful Work Immersion and livelihood experiences while developing their soft, hard, technological, functional, and employability skills toward greater independence, participation, and future employment or entrepreneurship.

1. To establish and sustain an inclusive school-based simulated workplace for accessible Work Immersion and livelihood training.

2. To engage SNED learners in appropriate café tasks such as food preparation, sanitation, customer service, teamwork, communication, and entrepreneurship based on their abilities.

3. To develop functional and employability skills through regular hands-on workplace routines and responsibilities.

4. To generate and reinvest income for ingredients, materials, supplies, and other SNED skills-development activities.

5. To provide an inclusive Work Immersion alternative that addresses accessibility, supervision, and industry-placement barriers while promoting independence and employability.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was SNED Café established?
SNED Café was established in December 2023, initially operating as a simple café booth before developing into a physical café environment.
What is the main purpose of SNED Café?
Its main purpose is to provide accessible and meaningful Work Immersion experiences while developing learners’ soft skills, hard skills, technological skills, and employability.
Do learners participate in activities outside the school?
Yes. Learners provide refreshments during Division-level events, such as Teachers’ Day celebrations, and benchmarking activities, allowing them to practice their skills in authentic settings.
How does SNED Café involve the community?
Learners purchase ingredients from nearby stores and interact with local businesses, including bakeries, to learn about food production, baking, purchasing, and business operations.
How does SNED Café benefit learners’ families?
The project provides learners with opportunities to earn from their skills, while proceeds may also help support educational needs, including graduation-related expenses, helping ease the financial burden on families.
