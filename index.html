Welcome to SNED CAFE

Welcome to SNED CAFE, the official coffee shop and pastry corner of FGNMHS Senior High School. We believe that great ideas are born over great coffee. Our mission is to provide students, teachers, and guests with premium, handcrafted beverages and freshly baked pastries in a warm, welcoming environment.

Every cup we serve is made with love, and every pastry is crafted to perfection. Whether you need a quick morning boost before class, a cozy spot to study, or a place to unwind, SNED CAFE is here for you.
